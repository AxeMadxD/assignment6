Assignment 6

jQuery: selectors, effects, animations

AITU students met at a birthday party. The guests stood in four rooms from 19:30 to 22:30, and at 21:30 the cake disappeared from the kitchen. The whole house is built from one array, and every case is a jQuery chain that searches the page.
